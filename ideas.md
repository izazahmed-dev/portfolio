# Design direction: The Evidence Edition

## Theme name

**The Evidence Edition** — a letterpress portfolio that treats shipped work as the headline and academic records as supporting proof.

## Design system

- **Movement:** editorial Swiss/letterpress hybrid; dense instrument panels balanced by generous paper fields.
- **Core principles:** proof before adjectives, one strong accent, varied section rhythms, no decorative motion without a reading purpose.
- **Color philosophy:** ink-black and warm paper for trust; registration red for actions, progress, and verified emphasis.
- **Layout paradigm:** a fixed running head, wide editorial shell, asymmetrical case studies, and evidence tables that feel like a printed register.
- **Signature elements:** registration marks, ink rules, document plates, measured figures, and a progress rule that doubles as navigation feedback.
- **Interaction philosophy:** every interactive element should reveal context before opening it; every overlay traps focus and returns it to its trigger.
- **Animation:** restrained GSAP reveals and Lenis smoothing, always skipped for reduced-motion users; movement should feel like ink settling onto paper.
- **Typography:** Bricolage Grotesque for expressive display, Instrument Sans for readable narrative, JetBrains Mono for numbers and references.
- **Brand essence:** precise, curious, and unusually honest for an early-career portfolio.
- **Brand voice:** direct, evidence-led, quietly confident; never inflate participation into achievement.
- **Wordmark/logo:** the name acts as the wordmark; registration marks and the red accent act as the identity system.
- **Signature brand color:** registration red `#e63946` in the dark run and `#d2320f` in the light run.

## Recruiter journey

The first screen should answer three questions quickly: who Izaz is, what he has built, and what action a visitor can take. The project dossier follows immediately after the title page; certificates and transcripts remain available as a differentiator, not as the opening argument.
